Privacy policy

Updated October 18, 2026

  • Dead Reckoner collects no data. There is no account, no usage statistics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • What you type (trips, weight and balance, settings, practice progress) is saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. We never see it. To delete it, use Settings → Erase my data, or delete the app.
  • The app works offline. It goes online only when you buy or restore Dead Reckoner Pro, which Apple handles (we receive no payment or personal details; Apple’s privacy policy applies), or when you open a link, which opens in Safari.
  • Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared or sold, to anyone, for any purpose. That includes children.
  • If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
  • Questions: info@hocusbuzz.com. Dead Reckoner is made by HocusBuzz in San Marcos, California.
