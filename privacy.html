Privacy policy

Updated November 9, 2026

  • Dead Reckoner collects no data. There is no account, no usage statistics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • What you type (trips, weight and balance, settings, practice progress) is saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. We never see it. To delete it, use Settings → Erase my data, or delete the app.
  • If you use the tools in GPS & compass or tap Variation at my position, the app asks to use your location. It uses it only on your iPhone, while you need it: to show your position, GPS track and groundspeed, to work out magnetic variation and to run the GPS trainers. Your location is never sent anywhere. A position is kept only when you save one yourself (a spot to go back to, the virtual VOR you place, the start of a DR check leg), and then only in the app on your iPhone, like the rest of your data. You can switch location off at any time in the iPhone’s Settings.
  • The app works offline. Its only connection is to Apple’s App Store, to show the price of Dead Reckoner Pro, check whether you own it, and buy or restore it. Apple handles this (we receive no payment or personal details; Apple’s privacy policy applies). Links open in Safari.
  • Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared or sold, to anyone, for any purpose. That includes children.
  • If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
  • Questions: info@hocusbuzz.com. Dead Reckoner is made by HocusBuzz in San Marcos, California.
